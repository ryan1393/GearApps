Recurring Fort Wayne RXO export window

Active load window: yesterday, today and tomorrow in Fort Wayne time. Exports below RXO's row limit are kept in full. Larger Orders and Shipments exports use verified date ranges to cover this window: Orders use Pickup or Delivery Date by direction; Shipments use the corresponding Appointment and Estimated Arrival dates. Freight On The Move stays live. Scans never overlap. If a scan runs past the maximum duration or stops producing output, the scheduler stops the exporter, waits for the recovery cooldown and retries once. Existing data stays available until a replacement batch validates.
